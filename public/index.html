Guide

A manual waitlist, with automatic follow-up.

Offering an opening

Enter the service, stylist and time. Clients must match the service, required stylist and full appointment window. The oldest eligible request gets the first offer.

Each offer lasts 15 minutes. A decline or timeout advances the queue. An acceptance fills the opening; staff then update Square. Late and duplicate replies cannot claim it.

Staff controls

Cancel an opening to withdraw its offer. If delivery fails, retry or skip the client. Opting out ends a pending offer and stops future texts. Declining or timing out keeps the client on the waitlist.

Hours and recovery

Outreach runs Tuesday–Saturday, 9 AM–6 PM Phoenix. Outside hours, it waits until reopening. Outreach stops at the appointment start. Restarting the worker preserves the current client and original deadline.

Testing the prototype

Choose “Demo” for 20-second offers and simulated open hours. Expand “Preview offer & test a reply” to accept, decline or opt out. Past offers stay available for late-reply testing. Edit a client to simulate a failed text.

No real texts are sent. Square updates and waitlist entry are manual.
